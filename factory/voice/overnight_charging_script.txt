Does charging your phone overnight kill its battery?
Mostly myth. But not completely.
Blame older nickel batteries. They had a "memory effect": recharge them before they ran low, and they could seem to lose capacity.
Your phone runs on lithium-ion, which has practically no memory effect.
First, most modern phones stop charging when full, then just top up now and then.
Many can even learn your routine, hold around eighty percent, then finish before you unplug.
Second, the real enemy: heat.
High temperatures speed up permanent capacity loss, and sitting at a full charge for hours adds extra stress.
Worst combo? Full and hot, like a charging phone under a pillow, where heat gets trapped.
The verdict: the night isn't the villain. Heat and long hours at full charge are.
