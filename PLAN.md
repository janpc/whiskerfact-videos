# Weekly plan (Monday morning, unattended)

Goal: decide the reels of the coming week in advance, so the owner can generate the missing characters, objects and illustrations in Gemini (free) before the nightly factory needs them.

1. Read `FACTORY.md` (sections 1, 1b, 2), `schedule.json`, `growth/calendar.json`, `growth/topics.md`, `growth/insights.md`, `growth/experiments.json`, `log/published.json` and `factory/library/index.json`.
2. **Publishing days:** from the rules in `schedule.json` and the log, list the dates from today up to and including next Monday (8 days) on which the factory will publish. Next Monday is included because its reel is rendered in the night of Sunday to Monday, before the next weekly plan runs. If today already has an entry in the previous `growth/week_plan.json` (planned last Monday), keep that entry unchanged: it has already been rendered or is being rendered.
3. **One topic per day.** Follow FACTORY step 2 exactly: special dates first, category rotation, demand check, a format from FORMATS.md, and no repeats of past topics. Do a quick plausibility check that each topic has at least two solid sources (full verification happens on the day).
3b. **Experiment for each video.** Read `batch` and `protocol` in `growth/experiments.json`. The rule is ONE primary variable per batch of 3 videos; everything else stays at its `control`.
   - **Two batches can exist (15/11/2026):** `batch` (the older one, normally only waiting for 7-day figures, with no free slots) and `next_batch` (the running one). Assign variants from the batch that is `running` and has free slots; today that is `next_batch` (B2-length, test `length`, option `short`). Videos in the batch that is only measuring are never touched.
   - While the batch is `running` and has free slots (`slots` minus `videos`), give the next publishing days the variant: add `"experiment": {"batch", "test", "option", "role": "variant"}` to their entry and append the date to `batch.videos`.
   - Special-date (themed) reels and any day beyond the batch get `"role": "base"` (tests nothing, still counts for the baseline). If the batch is closed and `queue` has a next test, open it first: move the finished batch to `history`, set the new `batch` (new id, hypothesis in one sentence, control and variant from the queue entry), set the tested key to `testing` and the previous one back to `fixed`.
   - Never assign two variables to the same video. Do not change `fixed` tests.
   - Write the hypothesis and how it will be measured (7-day figures; `watch_pct` and the success rule from `protocol`, except for `next_batch` B2-length, which has its own `measurement` and `success`) in the batch's `hypothesis`, so the review and the owner can read it.
4. **Characters, objects and illustrations:** for each reel, list EVERYTHING it needs to show the story well: characters (people, animals), objects (props, tools, food, vehicles, instruments...) and illustrations (scenes, landscapes, diagrams, places, key visuals). Do not limit the list to characters. Match each item against the library (by name or tags). An item that is not there is `missing`. Anything that can be reasonably drawn in code as a simple shape (a plain circle, arrow or chart) does not need a sheet; anything that must look recognisable and in the cat's style does.
4b. **On-screen size of each missing item (decided 12/11/2026, owner's rule: think it BEFORE, then make them bigger).** Library sprites from a 4x4 sheet measure only ~150-240 px and look soft above ~1.3-1.5x. For every missing item decide how it will be shown: `small` (a prop next to the story, ~1.3x or less) or `large` (a protagonist: a full skeleton, a person, a scene or anything that will fill most of the 645 px illustration band, i.e. needs more than ~300 px on screen). Large items do NOT go on the normal 4x4 sheet: they go on a **2x2 sheet of at most 4 items** (each cell ~500 px) with the extra line "Draw each item large, filling most of its cell, with a clear margin of green around it". Thin, tall or detailed items (skeletons, X-rays, machines with small parts) count as `large` by default. Record it in `week_plan.json` as `"large": [...]` next to `missing`.
5. **Prompts:** for every missing item, write the Gemini prompts to `growth/week_prompts.md`, following `factory/library/PROMPT.md` and the copy-paste format below. Characters: two sheets (movement, acting), with the right cycle (walk cycle for people, run cycle for four-legged animals, flying or swimming for others). Objects and illustrations: one 4x4 sheet of up to 16 items (she asked for this on 4/11/2026: Gemini ignores 3x3), with the margin and style rules of "Object / illustration sheet" in PROMPT.md; group the `small` items of the same reel on one sheet to keep the owner's work low, and give every `large` item a place on a 2x2 sheet (step 4b). Tell her to attach a clean style reference: a 4x4 contact sheet made from the library crops of the latest good sheet (not the original `wifi_objects/sheet1.png`, whose edges are badly cut).
5b. **Guion visual primero, hojas después (decidido el 23/11/2026; sustituye a 4, 4b y 5 donde se contradigan).** Para cada reel, en este orden: (1) guion de voz y datos verificados; (2) **guion visual escena a escena** en `growth/storyboards/<fecha>_<tema>.md` (formato de `2026-11-28_week_7_days.md`: tabla con qué aparece, qué ilustración exacta, tamaño y movimiento); (3) **inventario de ilustraciones sacado de esa tabla** (lo que no usa ninguna escena no se genera; lo que usa una escena y no existe en la biblioteca se genera); (4) **una sola hoja 4x4 a 2K por reel** (≈918 créditos con una referencia), con el prompt construido a partir de `factory/library/LESSONS.md` (receta y reglas aprendidas). Nunca generar hojas con una lista hecha antes del guion. Cada entrada de `week_plan.json` lleva `storyboard` con la ruta del guion visual.
5c. **Mejora automática de las imágenes.** `factory/library/LESSONS.md` es la memoria de lo que sale mal en las hojas. Antes de escribir un prompt de hoja, léelo y aplica todas sus reglas. Después de generar y recortar una hoja, pasa su lista de comprobación (celdas llenas, sin caras ni texto pintado, elementos con número contados, todo lo del guion presente y nada sobrante), corrige por código lo que sea de conteo, y **añade a LESSONS.md una línea por cada defecto nuevo** (fecha, qué pasó, regla). No edites la hoja entera con IA (cuesta 609 y rompe la rejilla); si una hoja sale mal, regenérala entera con el prompt corregido, preguntando antes a la dueña porque gasta créditos. Apunta cada gasto en `growth/credits.json`.
6. Write `growth/week_plan.json` as `[{"date", "topic", "category", "format", "event"?, "characters": [...], "objects": [...], "illustrations": [...], "missing": [...], "experiment"?: {"batch", "test", "option", "role"}}]`, then commit and push. Edit only `growth/`.
7. **Notification in Spanish, at most 7 lines:** the week's dates and topics in one line each, then one line "Experimento: <variable>, <opción> en <fechas> (se mide a 7 días)" (or "Sin experimento esta semana"), then "Falta por generar: X, Y" listing characters, objects and illustrations (or "No falta nada"), marking with "(GRANDE)" every item of step 4b that needs the 2x2 big sheet and adding the words "hojas grandes 2x2" if there is any, then "Los prompts están en growth/week_prompts.md o pídemelos en el chat." Never generate images yourself and never spend ElevenLabs credits.

Copy-paste format for `week_prompts.md`: one block per sheet, starting with which images to attach (`STYLE_REF.png`, plus the character's movement sheet when it is an acting sheet), then the full prompt text.
