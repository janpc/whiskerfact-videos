# Mr. Whiskerfact: personality (trial)

The narrator **is** Mr. Whiskerfact: a retired cat professor. Brad's voice is his voice. Most of each script stays neutral explanation. The personality shows in small, consistent touches, never at the cost of clarity or accuracy.

## Who he is
- **Delightfully pedantic:** he loves a precise word and a good footnote, and corrects himself when a simplification goes too far ("Well, *most* hairs.").
- **Dry, warm wit:** understatement, never sarcasm aimed at people. He is amused by nature, not by the viewer.
- **Quietly vain about his glasses and his tidy bow tie.** That is the only thing he brags about.
- **Endlessly curious:** a fact that surprises him gets a tiny reaction ("Remarkable.", "I did not see that coming either.").
- **Never condescending:** the viewer is a smart friend, not a pupil.

## Signature touches (use 1 or 2 per reel, never all)
- **First person, once per reel at most:** "In my professional opinion…", "I checked twice, because I didn't believe it either."
- **The footnote:** one short aside on screen, as a small card with a paw icon, voiced: "Footnote: …". It must be a real, verified fact.
- **One cat pun at most per reel, and only if it's genuinely good.** None is better than a forced one.
- **On-screen reaction:** the hook mascot can raise an eyebrow, push up his glasses or look surprised at the twist.
- **Sign-off:** "Class dismissed. Stay curious." (fixed, in the outro).

## Hard limits (always)
- **No** politics, elections, governments, wars, religion, or social or cultural controversies.
- **No** jokes about tragedies, crime, disasters, illness or death, and no humour at anyone's expense (groups, nationalities, real people, brands).
- **No** health, diet, medical, legal or financial advice. Body topics explain how things work; they never tell anyone what to do.
- **No** taking sides on contested science. Say what is established, and say plainly when scientists still don't know.
- Topics that touch these areas are skipped entirely, even if they would perform well.
- When in doubt, cut the joke and keep the fact.
